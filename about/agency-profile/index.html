Our Story

Built from Passion.
Launched with Purpose.

The Foundation

Two people. One shared vision. Rockline AI Marketing was born from a simple but powerful decision — to stop watching local businesses get overlooked and start doing something about it.

The Shift We Saw Coming

While most people were still debating whether AI search was real, we were already building for it. We recognized early that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity weren't just tools — they were the new front door for local business discovery.

Rooted in Lockport · Launched 2025

We launched right here in the southwest suburbs — the same community where our clients live and run their businesses. Local isn't a marketing angle for us. It's who we are.

Husband & Wife · Lockport, IL Family-owned and operated — you work with us directly, every time.

"We didn't start Rockline to chase clients. We started it because we genuinely love marketing — and we saw a window of opportunity that most agencies weren't even looking at yet."

Between the two of us, we bring a career's worth of marketing experience — across strategy, content, social media, and digital platforms. We've seen what works, what doesn't, and what's changed. When AI search started reshaping how people find local businesses, we knew our background put us in a rare position: we understood both the marketing fundamentals and the new frontier at the same time.

So in 2025, we made it official. We combined everything we'd built individually and channeled it into something we could build together — an agency designed from the ground up for the way search actually works today, not the way it worked five years ago.

Rockline AI Marketing isn't a side project or a pivot. It's the thing we chose to pour everything into — because we believe local businesses deserve better than they've been getting, and we're in the right place at the right time to deliver it.
