Agency Profile

Built from passion.
Launched with purpose.

The Foundation

Two people. One shared vision. Rockline AI Marketing was born from a simple but powerful decision — to help small business owners transition into the next level of marketing before their competitors.

The Shift We Saw Coming

While most people were still debating whether AI search was real, we were already building for it. We recognized early that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity weren't just tools — they were the new front door for local business discovery.

Rooted in Lockport · Launched 2025

We launched right here in the southwest suburbs — the same community where our clients live and run their businesses. Local isn't a marketing angle for us. It's who we are.

Husband & Wife · Lockport, IL Family-owned and operated — you work with us directly, every time.

"We didn't start Rockline to chase clients. We started it because we genuinely love marketing — and we saw a window of opportunity that most agencies weren't even looking at yet."

Between the two of us, we bring a career's worth of marketing experience — across strategy, content, social media, and digital platforms. We've seen what works, what doesn't, and what's changed. When AI search started reshaping how people find local businesses, we knew our background put us in a rare position: we understood both the marketing fundamentals and the new frontier at the same time.

So in 2025, we made it official. We combined everything we'd built individually and channeled it into something we could build together — an agency designed from the ground up for the way search actually works today, not the way it worked five years ago.

Rockline AI Marketing isn't a side project or a pivot. It's the thing we chose to pour everything into — because we believe local businesses deserve better than they've been getting, and we're in the right place at the right time to deliver it.

Who we serve

If you serve local customers, we can help you get found.

Rockline AI Marketing works with small and local businesses across Chicago's southwest suburbs. Our clients span a wide range of industries — what they all share is a need to be visible in the places their customers are actually looking, including AI-generated search results.

Home Services

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, roofers, landscapers, and contractors. When someone asks an AI "who's the best [trade] near me," we make sure your name is in the answer.

Restaurants & Food

Diners, cafes, catering companies, food trucks, and specialty food businesses. AI search is quickly becoming where people decide where to eat — we put you in that conversation.

Real Estate

Agents, brokers, and property managers. Buyers and sellers are asking AI for local expertise before they ever pick up the phone — your digital presence needs to answer them first.

Retail & Boutiques

Independent shops, specialty retailers, and local boutiques. We help brick-and-mortar businesses compete with national brands by owning their local AI footprint.

Professional Services

Law firms, financial advisors, accountants, medical and dental practices. When clients search for trusted local professionals, a strong GEO presence means your name comes up first.

Wellness & Health

Gyms, yoga studios, chiropractors, massage therapists, and wellness brands. People searching for local health services trust AI recommendations — we help you earn those mentions.

Don't see your industry? If you serve customers in a specific geographic area, GEO can work for you. Reach out and we'll give you an honest answer about what's possible.

Our plan for your success

Simple strategy. Powerful results.

We don't believe in complicated retainers, confusing packages, or strategies that take six months to explain. Our plan for your success comes down to three things done exceptionally well — and the way they work together is what makes all the difference.

01

A fully coded,
fully customizable website

Your website is the single most important asset in your digital presence — and most small business sites are holding their owners back. We build yours from scratch: clean code, no page builders, no templates that a hundred other businesses are also using.

Every site we build is structured for both traditional search engines and AI systems. That means proper semantic HTML, Schema.org markup, fast load times, and content architecture that large language models can actually read and trust. Your site becomes a citation-worthy source — not just a digital brochure.

Custom Coded GEO Structured Schema Markup Mobile First

02

Social content that builds
local authority

AI systems don't just read your website — they build a picture of your business from everything published about you across the web. Every post, article, and update is a data point. We create strategic social and microblog content that strengthens that picture consistently over time.

The goal isn't likes or followers. The goal is authority — making your business the most recognized, most referenced, and most recommended name in your local market, both by search engines and by the AI assistants your customers are already using.

Microblogging Brand Authority Local Signals AI Citations

03

Aerial & 8K photography
that stops the scroll

A great website and consistent content still need something worth looking at. We shoot drone photo and video, plus high-resolution 8K coverage of your property, storefront, or event — the kind of imagery a phone camera can't capture.

Those shots feed everything else in the plan: hero images on your site, thumbnails on social, and visual proof for real estate listings and event coverage. One shoot, used everywhere.

When these three pillars work together — a technically excellent website, a consistent stream of authority-building content, and imagery that actually shows off your business — you become the one that AI systems recognize, trust, and recommend. Not because you outspent your competitors. Because you out-positioned them. That's the Rockline AI Marketing plan, and it works for any local business that's ready to commit to it.
